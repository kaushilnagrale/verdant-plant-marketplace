## Light

Bright, indirect light for several hours a day - a succulent that
benefits from a sunny windowsill, though harsh midday direct sun can
scorch leaves that aren't acclimated to it.

## Water

A true succulent - water deeply, then let the soil dry out completely
before watering again, roughly every 2-3 weeks. Overwatering causes soft,
mushy, translucent leaves and root rot; this kills far more jade plants
than underwatering does.

## Common mistakes

Treating it like a regular houseplant and watering on a weekly schedule.
When in doubt, wait.

## Safety

Mildly toxic to cats and dogs if ingested - can cause vomiting,
incoordination, or a slowed heart rate in more serious cases. Keep away
from pets that chew on leaves.
