## Why a plant rather than cut flowers

A potted plant is commonly chosen over cut flowers for a romantic
occasion specifically because it lasts well beyond the day itself - a
living reminder rather than something that wilts within a week.

## Commonly given

**Rose (potted).** The obvious direct choice for this occasion, needing
a sunny balcony to actually thrive and rebloom.

**Jade Plant.** A lower-maintenance, longer-lived alternative for a
partner who wants something that survives without daily attention.

**Money Plant.** A safe, easy choice that grows well even in a shared
apartment with limited direct sun.

## What to avoid

No strong occasion-specific restrictions beyond general plant-gifting
cautions.
