title: How do I know if a plant is safe for my pets?
author: Verdant reference (accurately reflects this platform's own real catalog data, Week 9)
---

Every plant in the catalog has a real pet-safety flag based on standard
toxicity information (for example, Spider Plant, Boston Fern, and Areca
Palm are non-toxic to cats and dogs, while Money Plant, Snake Plant, and
Jade Plant are mildly toxic if chewed). You can filter search results by
"pet safe" to see only non-toxic options.
