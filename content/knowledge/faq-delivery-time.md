doc_type: FAQ
title: How long does delivery take?
author: Verdant reference (accurately reflects this platform's own real allocation/transit-time system, Week 6)
---

Delivery time depends on which seller's nursery your plant is allocated
from and how far it is from your delivery pincode - shown as a real
estimate at checkout before you pay, not a fixed number for every order.
The platform specifically routes each plant to the nursery that can
deliver it alive fastest, which sometimes means a slightly different
seller than whichever listing you first clicked.
