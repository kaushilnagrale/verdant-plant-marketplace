## Why a low-maintenance choice matters most

Someone recovering is not going to remember a watering schedule -
resilience matters more than novelty for this occasion.

## Commonly given

**Snake Plant.** Extremely forgiving of missed watering, a genuinely
practical choice while someone is unwell.

**Money Plant.** Similarly low-maintenance and easy to place bedside.

## What to avoid

Avoid anything needing daily attention (Boston Fern, Tulsi) - the whole
point of this gift is one less thing to worry about.
