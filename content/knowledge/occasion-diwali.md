## Why plants are given at Diwali

Diwali gifting is broad and plants are one common category alongside
sweets and diyas - flowering and fragrant plants are popular for
decorating the home during the festival itself.

## Commonly given

**Marigold.** Extremely closely associated with Diwali decoration -
garlands and potted marigolds are a common festive sight.

**Money Plant.** A popular general "prosperity" gift around Diwali,
similar to its role in housewarming gifts.

**Jade Plant.** Sometimes given specifically for its association with
prosperity in some traditions - regional and personal practice varies
considerably here.

## What to avoid

Nothing specific stands out as avoided for this occasion beyond the
general plant-gifting cautions (thorny plants, high-maintenance plants
for a traveling recipient).
