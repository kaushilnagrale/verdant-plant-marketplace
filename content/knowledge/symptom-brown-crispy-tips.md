## What it usually means

Brown, dry, crispy tips or edges (as opposed to soft, mushy brown spots)
almost always mean low humidity, underwatering, or tap water mineral
buildup - not a pest or disease.

## What to check

If the plant sits near an AC vent, heater, or in a very dry room, that's
the likely cause - move it or mist it regularly. If the soil is bone dry
more often than not, water more consistently. If tips brown despite
regular watering, try filtered or rested tap water for a few weeks.

## When it's something else

Crispy, curling leaves across the WHOLE plant (not just tips) combined
with dropped leaves points more toward underwatering generally, or in
winter, cold drafts.

## When to escalate

Crispy tips alone rarely mean the plant is dying - trim the dead tips
with clean scissors for appearance and correct the underlying humidity/
watering cause. Only escalate if new growth is also coming in damaged.
