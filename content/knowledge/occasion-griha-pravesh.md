## Why plants are commonly given at a housewarming

Plants are a widely recognized Griha Pravesh gift in India because they
represent growth and a home that is settling in, and are practical too -
the recipient has a new, often bare space to fill.

## Commonly given

**Tulsi.** Widely kept at the entrance or in a courtyard in many
households as part of a daily practice. Needs direct sun, so suits a home
with a balcony or terrace more than a fully indoor apartment.

**Money Plant.** Very commonly given for this occasion. Forgiving and
low-maintenance, grows in water or soil, and suits someone who has never
kept a plant before.

**Lucky Bamboo.** Often given in small arrangements. Suits an indoor
space with no direct light.

## What to avoid

Thorny plants are generally avoided for this occasion. High-maintenance
plants needing daily attention are a weaker choice if the recipient
travels often.
