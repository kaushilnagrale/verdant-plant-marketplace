## Why safety matters most for this occasion

A new baby in the house means the recipient is thinking about safety
first - pet and child safety (a plant within reach of a crawling infant)
matters more here than for almost any other occasion.

## Commonly given

**Spider Plant.** Non-toxic and easy, a genuinely safe choice for a home
with a new baby and possibly pets too.

**Areca Palm.** Non-toxic to pets and attractive, a popular safe choice
for a nursery or living room.

**Boston Fern.** Non-toxic and low-risk, though needs more humidity/
attention than the other two options here.

## What to avoid

Avoid anything on the toxic-to-pets/children list for this occasion
specifically - check the plant's own pet-safety fact before
recommending it here, more strictly than for other occasions.
