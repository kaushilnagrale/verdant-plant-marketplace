## Why a desk-sized, low-light plant is the safe default

An office desk rarely has direct sun and the recipient rarely has time
for daily care - the two constraints that matter most for this occasion.

## Commonly given

**Lucky Bamboo.** The most common desk gift for exactly this reason -
small, tolerates low light, minimal care.

**Money Plant (small pot).** Similarly forgiving and compact.

**Snake Plant (small pot).** Tolerates being forgotten for weeks, a real
advantage in a busy office.

## What to avoid

Avoid anything needing bright direct sun (Rose, Tulsi) - most desks
simply don't have it.
