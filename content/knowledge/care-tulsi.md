## Light

Needs direct sun for most of the day - traditionally kept in an open
courtyard or a bright balcony for exactly this reason. Struggles and
grows leggy in a shaded indoor corner.

## Water

Water daily in hot weather, checking that the soil doesn't stay
waterlogged - it likes consistent moisture but not standing water at the
roots.

## Common mistakes

Placing it in a low-light indoor spot for decoration - it needs real sun
to thrive, more than most other plants in this catalog.

## Safety

Non-toxic to cats and dogs, and edible for humans - commonly used
directly in cooking and herbal tea.
