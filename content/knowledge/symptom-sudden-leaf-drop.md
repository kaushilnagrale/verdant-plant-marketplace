## What it usually means

A plant that drops several leaves suddenly, without them yellowing or
browning first, is most often reacting to a sudden change - a cold
draft, a move between rooms, a long delivery journey, or a big
temperature swing - rather than a watering or pest problem.

## What to check

Think about what changed recently: a delivery in transit, being placed
near a door, window draft, or AC vent, or a recent repotting. Removing
the stressor is usually enough.

## When it's something else

If leaf drop is accompanied by yellowing first, treat it as the
yellowing-leaves guide instead (usually overwatering). If accompanied by
wilting, treat it as the drooping guide instead.

## When to escalate

A plant that keeps dropping healthy-looking leaves for more than 2-3
weeks after settling into a stable spot may have a root problem that
isn't visible yet - worth a check of the roots at the next repotting.
