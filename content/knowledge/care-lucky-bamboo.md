## Light

Low to bright indirect light - never direct sun, which scorches the
leaves. Suits an indoor desk or shelf with no direct window exposure.

## Water

If kept in water (the most common way it is sold and displayed), change
the water every 1-2 weeks and keep the roots fully submerged. If potted
in soil, keep the soil lightly moist, never soggy.

## Common mistakes

Using tap water with high chlorine/fluoride content for an extended
period - Lucky Bamboo is sensitive to this and can develop yellowing
leaf tips. Filtered or rested (left standing overnight) water is safer.

## Safety

Mildly toxic to cats and dogs if ingested. Note also: despite the name,
this is Dracaena sanderiana, not a true bamboo.
