## What it usually means

Fine, spider-web-like strands (especially near where leaves meet the
stem) combined with tiny yellow or white speckling on the leaf surface
is the classic sign of spider mites, a common houseplant pest that
thrives in dry indoor air.

## What to check

Look at the underside of leaves with a bright light or magnifier - tiny
moving specks (not always visible to the naked eye) confirm it. Isolate
the plant from others immediately, since mites spread easily.

## Generic treatment

Wipe leaves down with a damp cloth, increase humidity (mites dislike
moisture), and use a general insecticidal soap or neem oil spray,
reapplied weekly for a few weeks. See a nursery for a specific product
recommendation appropriate to the plant and your household (pets,
children).

## When to escalate

If webbing covers large portions of the plant or new growth is
already damaged, the infestation is advanced - isolate strictly and
consider professional treatment rather than home remedies alone.
