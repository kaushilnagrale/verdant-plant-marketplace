Tulsi (holy basil) holds a significant place in many Hindu households,
often kept in a dedicated pot in the courtyard or near the entrance and
tended daily as part of a family's regular practice. Its exact role and
the specific rituals around it vary considerably by region, family, and
tradition - this note describes a common, general pattern, not a
universal or authoritative one.
