## Light

Bright, indirect light - avoid direct sun, which scorches the fronds.

## Water

The least drought-tolerant plant in this catalog - keep the soil
consistently moist (not soggy) and mist the fronds regularly, especially
in dry heat or under air conditioning. Crispy, browning frond tips are
almost always low humidity or under-watering, not a disease.

## Common mistakes

Placing it near an AC vent or a heater - the dry air is the single most
common reason a Boston Fern struggles indoors, more so than light.

## Safety

Non-toxic to cats and dogs - one of the safest ferns to keep if you have
pets that nibble on leaves.
