## Light

Extremely tolerant - survives in low light corners and bathrooms with no
window, and also tolerates bright direct sun. One of the most forgiving
plants for a first-time owner.

## Water

Water sparingly - allow the soil to dry out completely between
waterings, roughly every 2-3 weeks indoors. This plant stores water in
its thick leaves; overwatering (root rot) is by far the most common way
to lose it, far more common than underwatering.

## Common mistakes

Watering on a fixed weekly schedule regardless of how dry the soil
actually is. Check the soil, not the calendar.

## Safety

Mildly toxic to cats and dogs if ingested (contains saponins) - can
cause nausea or vomiting. Keep away from pets that chew on leaves.
