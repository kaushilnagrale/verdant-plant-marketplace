## Light

Needs at least 5-6 hours of direct sun a day - a rose kept indoors away
from a window will struggle and produce few or no flowers. A balcony or
terrace in full sun suits it far better than an indoor corner.

## Water

Water deeply when the top inch of soil is dry, more often in hot
weather. Avoid wetting the leaves when watering (increases fungal
disease risk) - water at the base.

## Common mistakes

Keeping it as a purely indoor plant. It is a full-sun outdoor/balcony
plant that tolerates brief indoor display but will decline if kept
indoors permanently.

## Safety

Non-toxic to cats and dogs, though the thorns are a real, separate
hazard worth mentioning to a buyer with small children or pets that play
roughly.
