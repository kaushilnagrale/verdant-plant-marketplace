## What it usually means

Small, white, cotton-ball-like clusters, usually where leaves meet the
stem or along stem joints, are a classic sign of mealybugs, a common sap-
feeding pest.

## What to check

Look closely at leaf joints and the undersides of leaves. Sticky residue
on nearby leaves or the surface below the plant is also a sign (mealybug
"honeydew").

## Generic treatment

Dab visible clusters directly with a cotton swab dipped in rubbing
alcohol, then treat the whole plant with insecticidal soap or neem oil,
repeated weekly for several weeks since eggs hatch in waves. Isolate the
plant from others in the meantime.

## When to escalate

A heavy, widespread infestation across multiple stems, or one that
returns repeatedly after treatment, may need a stronger systemic
treatment - a nursery can advise on a product appropriate for the
specific plant.
