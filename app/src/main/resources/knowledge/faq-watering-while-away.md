title: How do I keep my plant alive while I'm travelling?
author: Verdant horticulture reference (AI-drafted from standard, widely published houseplant-care sources - not yet reviewed by an in-house horticulturist)
---

For trips under two weeks, a thorough watering right before you leave is
usually enough for most low/medium-water plants (Money Plant, Snake
Plant, Jade Plant) - move them away from direct sun and hot windows to
slow drying further. Boston Fern and Tulsi are the two exceptions in
this catalog that need more frequent water and are riskier to leave
unattended for more than a few days.
