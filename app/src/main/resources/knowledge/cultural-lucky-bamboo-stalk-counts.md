In Feng Shui-influenced gifting practice, the number of stalks in a
Lucky Bamboo arrangement is popularly said to carry different meanings -
commonly, 3 stalks for general good fortune, 5 for wealth, and 8 for
growth. Practices and specific meanings vary between sources, and this
should be treated as a popular convention rather than a single settled
authority.
