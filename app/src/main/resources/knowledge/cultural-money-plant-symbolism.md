The Money Plant (Epipremnum aureum / Pothos) is widely associated with
prosperity and good fortune in popular belief across parts of South and
East Asia, which is a major reason it's such a common general-purpose
gift - for a new home, a new job, a festival, or simply "just because".
This is a popular, widespread association rather than a claim rooted in
one specific, singular tradition.
