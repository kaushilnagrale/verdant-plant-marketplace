## Light

Bright, indirect light grows it fastest and produces the most "spiderette"
babies, but it tolerates lower light well too - a genuinely forgiving
plant for a beginner.

## Water

Water when the top inch of soil is dry, roughly weekly. Brown leaf tips
are usually a sign of fluoride/chlorine sensitivity from tap water or
inconsistent watering, not a serious problem.

## Common mistakes

Overcrowding the pot without repotting - this plant grows quickly and
produces plantlets that benefit from being separated and repotted every
year or two.

## Safety

Non-toxic to cats and dogs - a safe choice for pet owners, though cats
are sometimes strongly attracted to chewing the leaves (mildly
hallucinogenic to them, similar to catnip, not seriously harmful).
