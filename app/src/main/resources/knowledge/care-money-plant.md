## Light

Tolerates low light well, which is why it is one of the most commonly
gifted indoor plants - it survives in a corner with no direct sun. It
grows faster and the leaves develop brighter variegation in bright,
indirect light.

## Water

Water when the top inch of soil is dry, roughly weekly indoors. It also
grows happily rooted directly in a jar of water - this is a common,
supported way to keep it, not a compromise.

## Common mistakes

Overwatering is the most common way to lose this plant - yellowing
leaves are usually too much water, not too little. Let it dry out
between waterings rather than keeping the soil constantly damp.

## Safety

Epipremnum aureum is mildly toxic if chewed by cats or dogs (contains
insoluble calcium oxalates). Keep out of reach of pets that chew on
leaves.
