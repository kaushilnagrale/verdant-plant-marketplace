doc_type: FAQ
title: What if my plant arrives damaged or dead?
author: Verdant reference (accurately reflects this platform's own real claims process, Week 8)
---

If a plant arrives damaged or dead, you can file a claim within 72 hours
of delivery through your order page. The platform investigates what
actually happened (courier mishandling, packing, or weather) and
resolves it with a replacement, store credit, or refund depending on the
claim - you are not simply told to contact the seller directly and hope.
