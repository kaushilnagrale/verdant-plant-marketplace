## Why plants are given at Raksha Bandhan

Alongside the traditional rakhi and sweets, a small plant is an
increasingly common additional gift between siblings, valued as
something that lasts rather than being consumed.

## Commonly given

**Money Plant.** A common, safe, low-maintenance choice for a sibling
gift regardless of age.

**Lucky Bamboo.** A small desk-sized arrangement suits a sibling who
works in an office.

**Spider Plant.** A cheerful, easy, pet-safe option if the sibling has
pets at home.

## What to avoid

No strong occasion-specific restrictions - general plant-gifting
cautions apply (avoid high-maintenance plants for a sibling who travels
often).
